We cook Turkish food and give to charity.

The Turkish Society of Texas shares Turkish culture and cuisine through our cooking classes and cookbook, and raises money for charities in the US and Türkiye. We are a 501(c)(3) nonprofit public charity.

Become a memberWhat we do

Cooking classes and a cookbook

Our Turkish cooking classes and our cookbook are how it all started. They share our cuisine and have helped charities in both the USA and Türkiye.

Culture and education

We promote Turkish culture, history and traditions through educational programs.

The next generation

We pass Turkish traditions on to new generations of Turkish Americans.
