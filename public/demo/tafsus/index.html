Sixty years of Turkish American friendship in Philadelphia.

TAFSUS is a non-profit founded in 1966 to build understanding and friendship between the Turkish and American communities through educational, cultural and social activities. We serve Southeastern Pennsylvania, Delaware and Southern New Jersey.

What your membership makes possible

Republic Day Dinner Dance

Every autumn we celebrate the founding of the Turkish Republic with dinner, music and dancing. It is the biggest night of our year.

Every autumn

ATA School

Turkish language and culture classes for children aged 4 to 14 and for adults, so our children can enjoy their heritage as Turkish Americans. Jointly sponsored with the Middle East Center at UPenn.

Since 2008

Okumus/TAFSUS Fellowship

We send teachers and school administrators to Turkey for a two-week summer study tour. The World Affairs Council of Philadelphia selects the fellows on our behalf.

Since 1999

Culture in the city

Turkish nights at the Philadelphia Museum of Art, Turkish Delight days at the Penn Museum, concerts, lectures and film, often with partners such as the Alliance Francaise de Philadelphie.

Through the year

Getting together

Luncheons, holiday celebrations, sing-alongs with the TAFSUS Choir and our picnic at Ridley Creek State Park.

Through the year

From 1966 to today

  1. 1966

    TAFSUS is established.

  2. 1999

    The Okumus/TAFSUS Fellowship begins sending teachers to Turkey.

  3. 2008

    ATA School opens for children and adults.

  4. 2010

    Mayor Nutter proclaims May 29 Turkish American Friendship Day in Philadelphia.

  5. 2026

    Sixty years of TAFSUS.

Annual dues keep every one of these programs going.

Members receive email updates and discounts. Dues are what let TAFSUS keep the Turkish American community in the Philadelphia area connected.
