Turkish culture and community in central Ohio since 1969.

TAACO is one of the oldest Turkish American organizations in the United States. We share the best of Turkish culture and history with central Ohio, and we look after the social and cultural life of our community here.

Two doctors traveled to Chicago in 1968.

The first Turkish professionals came to Columbus in the mid 1950s for specialized training, expecting to stay a couple of years. By the late 1960s it was clear they had a new home.

In April 1968 two Turkish doctors traveled to Chicago to ask the Consulate General of Turkey whether they could become American citizens without severing ties to their motherland. The Consulate encouraged them to form an association. TAACO was founded in January 1969, and the first October 29 ball welcomed 129 guests.

We are a non-profit 501(c)(3) organization.
